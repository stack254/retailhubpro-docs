# Plans and Billing

Use the `Subscription` area to manage your RetailHub POS plan, review billing status, and renew or upgrade.

## Where to go

Open:

- `Subscription`

## Available plans

RetailHub POS offers tiered plans based on the number of stores, users, and products your business needs. Pricing is available in Kenya Shillings (KES) for Kenya and US Dollars (USD) for international accounts.

To view current plan options and pricing, open `Subscription` or visit the [pricing page](https://retailhubpro.com/#pricing).

## Billing periods

Each plan is available on a monthly or annual basis. Annual billing costs less than paying monthly over the same period.

## What your plan controls

- maximum number of stores
- maximum number of users
- maximum number of products
- access to advanced features

When you reach a plan limit, you will need to upgrade before adding more stores, users, or products.

## How to renew or upgrade

1. Open `Subscription`.
2. Review the current plan and expiry date.
3. Select the plan and billing period you want.
4. Choose your payment method.
5. Complete payment.
6. Your plan activates or extends after payment is confirmed.

## What happens when a plan expires

When your subscription expires:

- access to operational features is restricted
- your data is not deleted
- you can renew at any time to restore access

Renew before the expiry date to avoid any interruption.

## Billing currency

Your billing currency is determined by your registered business country. Kenya-based businesses are billed in KES. All other businesses are billed in USD.

## Good practice

- Check your expiry date regularly under `Subscription`
- Renew at least a few days before expiry to avoid any gap in access
- Verify that payment has completed and the subscription is active before the expiry date
- If a payment completes but the plan does not activate, contact support with your payment reference
