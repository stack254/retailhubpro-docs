# OnePOS+ Overview

OnePOS+ is the Android app companion to RetailHub POS. It lets your team sell from any Android phone while sharing the same store, products, and inventory.

## How it relates to RetailHub POS

OnePOS+ and RetailHub POS are two interfaces into the same system. Changes made in one are visible in the other:

- products added in RetailHub POS appear in OnePOS+
- stock sold through OnePOS+ reduces inventory visible in RetailHub POS
- reports include sales from both interfaces

## When to use OnePOS+

- **Floor staff** who move around the store and cannot use a fixed browser terminal
- **Pop-up or outdoor selling** where a laptop is impractical
- **Backup terminal** when the main browser POS is unavailable

## Offline capability

OnePOS+ is designed to continue working during short periods without internet. Transactions sync automatically when connectivity is restored.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet
- An active RetailHub POS account with a store assigned to the user
- An internet connection for the initial setup and sync
