# Reports and Subscription

This guide covers the `Reports` area and the `Subscription` area.

## Reports

Use reports to review store and business performance.

### What to review regularly

- sales trends
- profit and loss
- store performance
- transaction summaries

### Good practice

- review daily sales at the end of the day
- compare branch performance weekly
- use reports to verify that inventory and sales activity match expectations

## Subscription

Use the subscription area to manage plan access and billing status.

### What you can do

- view the current plan
- review available plans
- submit a renewal or upgrade request
- verify billing status after payment

### Good practice

- renew before expiry to avoid interrupted access
- verify the selected billing currency before payment
- check subscription notices if features become unavailable
