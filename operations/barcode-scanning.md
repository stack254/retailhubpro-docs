# Barcode Scanning

Use barcode scanning at the POS to add products to the cart quickly without typing.

## Two ways to scan

### Camera scanner (built-in)

Uses your device's camera to read barcodes through the browser. Works on laptops, tablets, and phones with a camera.

### USB or keyboard wedge scanner

A physical barcode scanner plugged into your device. When a product barcode is scanned, the code is entered automatically and the product is looked up.

## Using the camera scanner

1. Open `Sales` and start building a cart.
2. Click the barcode scanner icon in the POS header.
3. Grant camera access when the browser asks.
4. Point the camera at the product barcode.
5. Hold steady — the scanner detects and reads the barcode automatically.
6. The product is added to the cart.
7. Close the scanner to continue adding items or proceed to payment.

### Switching cameras

If your device has more than one camera, a dropdown appears in the scanner. Select `Back camera` or `Rear camera` for the best scan angle on mobile.

## Using a USB or keyboard wedge scanner

1. Open `Sales` and ensure the barcode input field is focused.
2. Scan the product barcode with the physical scanner.
3. The code is entered automatically and the product is looked up.
4. The product is added to the cart.

The input field auto-submits when a code of 8 to 14 digits is detected, so no additional key press is needed.

## Manual barcode entry

If scanning is not available:

1. Click the barcode input field.
2. Type the barcode number (8 to 14 digits).
3. Press `Enter`.
4. The product is looked up and added to the cart.

## When a barcode is not found

If the scan or entry returns no product:

- confirm the product exists in inventory
- check that stock is loaded for the current store
- verify the barcode is correctly assigned to the product in `Inventory`
- ensure the correct store context is selected

## Adding barcodes to products

Barcodes are assigned in `Inventory` when creating or editing a product. Enter the barcode number in the barcode field on the product form.

## Good practice

- Test barcode scanning on new products before going live
- Keep the camera lens clean for reliable reads
- Use a consistent barcode format across your product range
